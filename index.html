Five steps of increasing delegation, adapted from the supplied Boris Cherny “Steps of AI Adoption” excerpt. The framework mapping and transition guidance are our interpretation, not an official equivalence or certification.

Different lenses. These steps describe delegation, not architectural certification. Azure has a five-level workload maturity model; GitHub includes a governance maturity assessment. Both guide design and ongoing improvement. This table suggests emphasis, not equivalent maturity levels or permission to increase autonomy.
